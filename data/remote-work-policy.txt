Acme Robotics — Remote Work Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: People Operations

1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules and expectations for employees who work
remotely, either on a full-time or hybrid basis. It applies to all
full-time employees who have received written approval from their
manager and People Operations to work outside of a company office.

2. Eligibility

Employees become eligible for remote work after completing 90 days of
employment and receiving a satisfactory performance review. Roles that
require physical presence, such as hardware lab technicians and site
security staff, are not eligible for remote arrangements.

3. Work Hours and Availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during the core hours of
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their assigned time zone. Outside of core hours,
employees may set their own schedule as long as they meet weekly
deliverables agreed upon with their manager.

4. Equipment and Expenses

The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time home office
stipend of $500 to every approved remote employee. Employees are
responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection of at least
25 Mbps download speed. Internet reimbursement of up to $50 per month
is available upon submission of a receipt.

5. Security Requirements

All remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal
systems, enable full-disk encryption on their work laptop, and lock
their screen whenever they step away from their workstation. Personal
devices may not be used to access customer data under any
circumstances.

6. Performance Expectations

Remote work does not change an employee's performance expectations.
Managers will continue to evaluate remote employees using the same
quarterly OKR process used for in-office staff. Employees who
consistently miss deadlines or are unreachable during core hours may
have their remote work privileges reviewed or revoked.

7. Travel to Headquarters

Remote employees are expected to travel to company headquarters in
Austin, Texas at least twice per year for team planning sessions. The
company covers reasonable travel costs, including economy airfare,
hotel, and a daily meal allowance of $75.

8. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations in collaboration
with department heads. Employees may propose changes by submitting
feedback through the internal People Ops portal.

For questions about this policy, contact people-ops@acmerobotics.example.
